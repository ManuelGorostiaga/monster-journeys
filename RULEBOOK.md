# Monster Journeys — Official Rulebook

**A Tabletop Board Game of Exploration, Monster Catching, and Tactical Combat**  
*For 1 to 4 players.*

---

### Introduction & Premise

In **Monster Journeys**, you take on the role of an ambitious trainer exploring an uncharted region of diverse biomes: dense forests, rugged mountains, open plains, winding freshwater rivers, and roaring ocean waters. Throughout your expedition, you will lay down territory cards to discover new routes, track and capture wild elemental monsters, learn ancestral combat techniques, purchase strategic equipment from the river market, and challenge adaptive Gym Leaders.

Your ultimate goal is to assemble a balanced team, collect Gym Badges, awaken legendary expansions, and triumph in the climactic **Grand Tournament** to become the regional Champion.

### 1. Components

* **1 Regional Node Map** consisting of **17 interconnected location nodes**:
  * **4 Starting Corner Nodes** (Capacity: 1 card each):
    * Southwest (SW): *Ciudad Hoja* (Leaf City)
    * Northwest (NW): *Ciudad Pico* (Peak City)
    * Northeast (NE): *Ciudad Puerto* (Port City)
    * Southeast (SE): *Ciudad Flama* (Flame City)
  * **3 Central Crossroads / Hubs** (Capacity: up to 2 cards each):
    * *Encrucijada Poniente* (West Crossroads)
    * *Gran Plaza Central* (Grand Central Plaza)
    * *Cruce del Alba* (Dawn Crossing)
  * **10 Regional Route & Dead-End Nodes** (Capacity: 1 card each):
    * *Cueva Origen*, *Valle Rocoso*, *Fosa Abisal*, *Ruta Poniente*, *Bahía Bruma*, *Ruta Septentrional*, *Pico Nevado*, *Meseta Oriental*, *Ruta Marina Este*, *Arrecife del Sol*.

  * *Ciudad Hoja* (Herbaceous Gym, Leader Erika)
  * *Ciudad Pico* (Summit Gym, Leader Brock)
  * *Ciudad Puerto* (Surge Gym, Leader Misty)
  * *Ciudad Flama* (Volcano Gym, Leader Blaine)
* **17 Environment Cards** across 5 distinct biomes:
  * **4 Forest Cards (Bosque)**: *Bosque de las Bayas*, *Bazar del Claro*, *Arboleda de Sabiduría*, *Santuario del Druida*.
  * **4 Mountain Cards (Montaña)**: *Mina de Piedra Oro*, *Refugio de los Picos*, *Nido del Águila*, *Cueva de los Fósiles*.
  * **4 Plains Cards (Pradera)**: *Molino de los Vientos*, *Feria Ambulante*, *Establo de los Relámpagos*, *Llanura de Duelos*.
  * **3 Freshwater Cards (Agua Dulce)**: *Cascada Cristalina*, *Muelle del Pescador*, *Laguna de los Lotos*.
  * **2 Saltwater Cards (Agua Salada — Tier 1)**: *Arrecife Resplandeciente*, *Navío Naufragado*.
  * **1 Saltwater Special Card (Agua Salada — Tier 3 Expansion)**: *Vórtice Legendario*.

#### 1.3 Monster Cards (50 Cards Total)
Monsters are categorized into 4 capture tiers (Tiers 1 to 4) and 10 elemental types (*Grass, Fire, Water, Electric, Ground, Flying, Rock, Bug, Dragon, Ice*):
* **40 Standard Monsters** (8 per biome: 2 Tier 1, 2 Tier 2, 4 Tier 3).
* **10 Tier 4 Legendary Monsters** (Awakened via the Tier 4 expansion): *Yggdrassor (Grass), Soberanocoris (Bug), Geotitán (Rock), Terragón (Ground), Aethelgard (Flying), Glaciodón (Ice), Fulgoraptor (Electric), Piroclasto (Fire), Hydragón Marino (Water), Bahamut Abisal (Dragon)*.

#### 1.4 Item Cards (20 Cards Total)
* **4 Capture Tools**: *Pokeball Básica* (Tier 1, Cost 4), *Superball Reforzada* (Tier 2, Cost 8), *Ultraball Avanzada* (Tier 3, Cost 14), *Masterball Suprema* (Tier 4, Cost 22).
* **9 Combat Items**: *Banda Fuerza* (Cost 6), *Chaleco Defensivo* (Cost 6), *Garra Rápida* (Cost 5), *Cinta Focus* (Cost 8), *Lente Preciso* (Cost 7), *Colmillo Afilado* (Cost 6), *Baya Aranja / Oran Berry* (Cost 4), *Casco Dentado* (Cost 6), *Pesa Recia* (Cost 7).
* **7 Utility Items**: *Botas Ligeras* (Cost 5), *Incienso Silvestre* (Cost 5), *Mapa de Rutas* (Cost 5), *Flauta Mágica* (Cost 6), *Moneda Amuleto* (Cost 6), *Telescopio Explorador* (Cost 7), *Imán de Tesoros* (Cost 7).

#### 1.5 Character Cards & Player Materials
* **4 Character Cards**:
  * **Red (El Audaz)**: Starts with 2 Coins, 3 Luck, *Candilín* (Fire Starter), *Pokeball Básica*, *Baya Aranja*. Ability: *Determinación Ardiente* (+1 damage when current HP $\le$ 50%).
  * **Blue (El Estratega)**: Starts with 2 Coins, 4 Luck, *Renacuajito* (Water Starter), *Superball Reforzada*, *Banda Fuerza*. Ability: *Primer Golpe & Fortuna* (+2 Speed on turn 1 of combat; utility shop items cost 1 less coin).
  * **Marina (La Pescadora)**: Starts with 4 Coins, 3 Luck, *Patiloto* (Water Starter), *Pokeball Básica*, *Botas Ligeras*. Ability: *Navegante Experta* (Normal movement in water biomes; visiting a water node grants +1 Luck).
  * **Flint (El Montañero)**: Starts with 3 Coins, 3 Luck, *Hojarín* (Grass Starter), *Pokeball Básica*, *Chaleco Defensivo*. Ability: *Resistencia Rocosa* (+2 Maximum HP to all monsters on your team).
* **4 Player Pawns** (Red, Blue, Green, Purple).
* **Personal Player Boards** (Each holds up to 4 monsters: 3 active party members + 1 reserve).

#### 1.6 Dice, Tokens & Markers
* **Combat Dice**: Six-sided dice with custom values: **[0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2]**.
* **Gold Coin Tokens (🪙)**: The regional currency.
* **Luck Tokens (🍀)**: Reroll and modifier tokens.
* **4 Gym Badges**: *Medalla Hoja*, *Medalla Risco*, *Medalla Cascada*, *Medalla Volcán*.
* **Round Marker**: Tracks rounds 1 through 10.

### 2. Setting Up the Game

Follow these numbered steps in physical order:

1. **Place the Regional Board**: Lay the regional node map in the center of the table.
2. **Plant Starting Corner Cities**: Place the 4 Gym City cards on the 4 corner nodes of the board:
   * Southwest Corner (SW) $\rightarrow$ *Ciudad Hoja*
   * Northwest Corner (NW) $\rightarrow$ *Ciudad Pico*
   * Northeast Corner (NE) $\rightarrow$ *Ciudad Puerto*
   * Southeast Corner (SE) $\rightarrow$ *Ciudad Flama*
3. **Prepare the Expansion Reserves**:
   * Set aside the Tier 2, Tier 3, and Tier 4 Monster Cards into three separate face-down decks beside the board.
   * Set aside the Tier 3 Environment card (*Vórtice Legendario*) into the Tier 3 expansion reserve.
4. **Prepare the Initial Environment Deck**: Shuffle the remaining Tier 1 Environment Cards into a face-down draw deck.
5. **Prepare the 5 Biome Encounter Decks**: Group the Tier 1 monster cards by biome (*Forest, Mountain, Plains, Freshwater, Saltwater*). Shuffle each into its own face-down deck near the board.
6. **Establish the River Shop**:
   * Shuffle the 20 Item Cards.
   * Deal **exactly 8 unique item cards** face-up in a row to form the River Shop display. If a duplicate appears, return it to the deck, reshuffle, and draw until 8 unique cards are displayed. Place the remaining cards face-down as the Item Draw Deck.
7. **Player Setup**:
   * Each player chooses or draws 1 Character Card.
   * Take the matching colored player pawn.
   * Take the starting Gold Coins and Luck Tokens listed on your Character Card.
   * Take your character's designated Starter Monster and starting items into your personal inventory.
   * Place your starter monster in the first active slot of your personal board.
8. **Deal Starting Location Hands**: Deal **5 cards** from the Environment Deck to each player. This forms your private hand of territory cards.
9. **Place Starting Pawns**:
   * Player 1 places their pawn on the SW Corner (*Ciudad Hoja*).
   * Player 2 places their pawn on the NE Corner (*Ciudad Puerto*).
   * Player 3 (if playing) places their pawn on the NW Corner (*Ciudad Pico*).
   * Player 4 (if playing) places their pawn on the SE Corner (*Ciudad Flama*).
10. **Set the Round Marker**: Place the Round Marker on space 1 of the round track. Player 1 takes the first turn.

---

### 3. Object of the Game

In Monster Journeys, you compete to assemble the strongest battle team and explore the region. You achieve this by:
* Discovering new biomes and creating strategic travel connections.
* Capturing wild creatures and equipping them with tactical items.
* Defeating Gym Leaders to unlock more powerful monsters.

#### Endgame Triggers & Victory
The exploration phase of the game ends immediately upon either of two events:
1. **Any player defeats their 3rd Gym Leader** and collects 3 Gym Badges.
2. **Round 10 concludes**.

When either trigger occurs, regular exploration ceases and all players enter the **Grand Tournament**. The player who wins the tournament is declared the regional Champion and wins the game.

---

### 4. Game Round

The game is played over a maximum of **10 rounds**. Each round is divided into two phases:

1. **Player Turns**: Beginning with Player 1 and proceeding in clockwise order, each player executes their full turn.
2. **Round Maintenance**: Once all players have completed their turns:
   * Advance the Round Marker by 1 space on the round track.
   * If the marker moves past space 10, the exploration phase ends immediately and the Grand Tournament begins (see [Section 8](#8-the-grand-tournament-and-determining-the-champion)).
   * Otherwise, the next round begins with Player 1.

---

### 5. Player's Turn

On your turn, execute the following steps in strict order:

* **5.1 Start of Turn Maintenance**
* **5.2 Movement & Map Placement**
* **5.3 Location Action (Optional)**
* **5.4 Main Action: Encounter, Gym, or Shop (Concludes the turn)**
* **5.5 Turn Conclusion**

---

#### 5.1 Start of Turn Maintenance
1. **Collect Passive Incomes**: Resolve start-of-turn effects from any items you possess.
   > **Example**: If you possess the *Imán de Tesoros* (Treasure Magnet), receive 1 Gold Coin (🪙) from the supply.
2. **Reset Movement Points**: Gain **1 Movement Point** for the turn.
   > **Exception**: If you hold or have equipped the *Botas Ligeras* (Light Boots), your base movement for the turn is **2 Movement Points**.
3. **Refill Location Hand**: If you have fewer than 5 cards in your location hand, draw cards from the Environment Deck until your hand holds **exactly 5 cards**.
   * **Luck Draft Mode**: If you have at least 1 Luck Token (🍀), you may spend 1 to reveal the top 3 cards of the Environment Deck, choose 1 for your hand, and discard the other 2. This action is taken in place of drawing normally.

---

#### 5.2 Movement & Map Placement
During movement, choose one of the following options using your available movement points:

##### Option A: Travel Existing Routes
Move your pawn along connected path lines to any discovered node (a node already containing at least 1 card). Each step costs 1 Movement Point. You cannot enter empty nodes using this option.

##### Option B: Discover a New Zone (Place Location Card)
If your pawn is connected by a line to an empty node (or a crossroads with available capacity):
1. Choose 1 location card from your hand.
2. Place it face-up on the destination node.
3. Move your pawn onto that new card, spending 1 Movement Point.
4. **Luck Reward**: If the node is marked with a **+1 🍀**, immediately gain **+1 Luck Token (🍀)**.
5. If you possess the *Mapa de Rutas* (Route Map), immediately gain **+1 Gold Coin (🪙)**.

##### Option C: Fast Travel Between Cities
If your pawn is currently on any City node (*Ciudad Hoja, Ciudad Pico, Ciudad Puerto, Ciudad Flama*):
* You may spend your movement to fast-travel directly to any other discovered City, regardless of distance along path lines.
* Fast-traveling between cities consumes both your movement and your action for the turn.

##### Overworld Monster Abilities
Before or during your movement, you may activate the overworld ability of one of your active monsters:
* **Excavación (Ground)**: Gain +1 additional Movement Point for this turn.
* **Nado Veloz (Water)**: Movement is set to 2 when moving across water biomes (*Freshwater* or *Saltwater*).
* **Vuelo Rápido (Flying)**: Teleport directly to any discovered City from anywhere on the board.
* **Calor de Campamento (Fire)**: Restore 3 HP to your entire team and gain 1 Gold Coin.
* **Sobrecarga (Electric) / Romperrocas (Rock)**: Immediately gain 1 Gold Coin.
* **Rastreo Silvestre (Grass)**: Reveal 1 additional card during wild searches this turn.
* **Red de Seda (Bug)**: Your next capture this turn treats your capture tool tier as +1 higher.

---

#### 5.3 Location Action (Optional)
If the card on your current node features a special Location Action, you may resolve it at this time.

* **Single-Use Restriction**: Each specific location card's action may be used **only once per player per game**. Mark the card with your player token to record that you have claimed its benefit.

##### Notable Location Actions:
* **Descanso en Casa (Starting Town / Home)**: Fully heal all your monsters to 100% HP and gain +1 Luck (🍀).
* **Bosque de las Bayas**: Gain 1 free *Baya Aranja* (Oran Berry) card and +1 Luck (🍀).
* **Arboleda de Sabiduría**: Pay 1 Luck (🍀) to teach 1 Ancestral Attack (*Danza de Espinas, Ignición Mística, Chorro Torrencial, Electrochoque, Muralla de Granito, Veneno Arácnido*) to one of your creatures.
* **Santuario del Druida**: Permanently grant +1 Maximum HP to one of your monsters and gain 1 Gold Coin (🪙).
* **Muelle del Pescador (Travesía en Barca)**: Sail directly to any discovered node on the map.
* **Feria Ambulante**: Opens the River Shop immediately with a 1-coin discount on all items.
* **Vórtice Legendario (Tier 3 Mythic Area)**: Pay 2 Luck (🍀) to summon and search among the Tier 4 Legendary Monsters.
* **Trading Posts & Mines**: Exchange coins for luck or luck for coins as printed on the card.

---

#### 5.4 Main Action (Concludes the Turn)
After completing your movement and any optional location action, choose **one** of the following primary actions. Resolving it concludes your turn.

##### Action 1: Search Wild Monsters (Non-City Biomes Only)
You cannot search for wild monsters while in a City. In a wild biome (*Forest, Mountain, Plains, Freshwater, Saltwater*):
1. **Reveal Encounters**: Draw **3 monster cards** from the matching Biome Encounter Deck and lay them face-up.
   * Add +1 card if you activated a Grass overworld ability.
   * Add +1 card if you own the *Incienso Silvestre*.
   * Add +1 card if you own the *Telescopio Explorador*.
2. **Redraw Option (Luck)**: Before choosing, you may spend **1 Luck Token (🍀)** to discard all revealed monsters and draw a completely new set of cards.
3. **Select One Outcome**:
   * **Capture**: Choose 1 revealed monster to capture. You must possess a Capture Tool in your inventory whose Tier is equal to or greater than the monster's Capture Tier:
     * *Pokeball Básica*: Captures Tier 1.
     * *Superball Reforzada*: Captures Tier 1 and 2.
     * *Ultraball Avanzada*: Captures Tier 1, 2, and 3.
     * *Masterball Suprema*: Captures any creature (Tiers 1 to 4).
     * *Placement*: Place the captured monster onto your personal board (up to 4 monsters: 3 active + 1 reserve). If your board already holds 4 monsters, send it to your personal storage.
   * **Teach an Attack**: Choose 1 revealed wild monster. You may teach its primary attack directly to any creature on your team. Each creature can know up to 3 attacks. If it already knows 3, replace the second attack.
   * **Flee / Pass**: Conclude the encounter without capturing or learning moves.

##### Action 2: Challenge a Gym Leader (City Nodes Only)
While standing in a City whose Gym you have not yet defeated:
1. Announce your challenge to the resident Gym Leader.
2. **Adaptive Gym Scaling**: Gym Leaders adjust their lineup strength based on how many total Gym Badges you currently possess:
   * **0 Badges Defeated**: The Leader fields a **Tier 1 Team** (2 Tier 1 monsters). Victory reward: **3 Coins, 1 Luck, Gym Badge**.
   * **1 Badge Defeated**: The Leader fields a **Tier 2 Team** (2 Tier 2 monsters). Victory reward: **4 Coins, 1 Luck, Gym Badge**.
   * **2 Badges Defeated**: The Leader fields a **Tier 3 Team** (3 Tier 3 monsters). Victory reward: **6 Coins, 2 Luck, Gym Badge**.
3. **Resolve Combat**: Conduct the battle following the rules in [Section 5.5](#55-1v1-combat-system).
4. **Resolution & Rewards**:
   * **If Victorious**: Claim the designated Coins, Luck, and the Gym Badge.
   * **If Defeated**: You still collect the **full coin reward** of the battle to recover and rebuild your strategy!
   * **Global Expansion Unlocks**:
     * Defeating the world's 1st Gym unlocks the **Tier 2 Expansion** (Tier 2 monsters are shuffled into biome decks).
     * Defeating the world's 2nd Gym unlocks the **Tier 3 Expansion** (Tier 3 monsters and *Vórtice Legendario* enter play).
     * Defeating the world's 3rd Gym unlocks the **Tier 4 Expansion** (awakens the 10 Legendary Monsters).

##### OR Action 3: Visit the River Shop (City Nodes Only)
You may only access the shop if you are physically in a City (or activated *Feria Ambulante*).
1. **Purchase Items**: You may buy any of the 8 items visible in the River Shop by paying its Gold Coin cost.
2. **Instant Refill**: Each time an item is purchased, immediately draw a card from the Item Deck to replace it. The shop must always maintain **exactly 8 unique items** without duplicates.
3. **Equip Items**: You may freely equip, unequip, or swap items between your inventory and your monsters outside of battle:
   * Each creature may hold **at most 1 equipped item**.
   * Equipping *Pesa Recia* (+3 Max HP) immediately increases current and maximum HP by 3. Unequipping it reduces maximum HP by 3.

##### Action 4: Pass
If you cannot or choose not to take any other action, conclude your turn.

#### 5.5 1v1 Combat System
Combat is resolved in 1-on-1 duels between active monsters, with each side bringing a party of up to 3 monsters into the arena.

##### Step 1: Turn Decision
In each round of combat, choose either to **Attack** or to **Switch Monsters**:

##### Step 2A: Switching Monsters
* You may switch your active monster with any conscious bench monster in your active party of 3.
* **Switch Penalty**: Switching consumes your entire turn. The incoming monster enters the arena and **immediately receives the opponent's attack without dealing damage** that round.
  > **Exception**: Attacks with the **Pivot Switch** effect (*Voltiocambio, Ida y Vuelta, Giro Marino*) allow you to attack and switch immediately with zero damage penalty.

##### Step 2B: Attacking & Initiative
If you choose to attack, select 1 of your active monster's attacks.
1. **Check Initiative**: Compare the Speed attribute of both active creatures:
   * The monster with higher Speed attacks first. Ties are broken randomly.
   * **Paralysis**: A paralyzed monster has its Speed reduced to **0**.
   * **Equipment Modifiers**: *Garra Rápida* grants +2 Speed. Blue's passive grants +2 Speed on the first combat turn.

##### Step 3: Resolving an Attack
Total damage dealt is calculated using the formula:
$$\text{Total Damage} = \text{Base Damage} + \text{Combat Dice Roll} + \text{Bonuses} - \text{Defenses}$$

1. **Base Damage**: Stated on the attack card.
   * *Speed Damage*: If the attack has the `speed_damage` effect (*Electroimpulso, Giro Rápido*), base damage equals the attacker's Speed.
   * *Fixed Damage*: If the attack has the `fixed_damage` effect (*Furia Dragón, Fijación Sísmica*), it deals guaranteed damage and ignores dice rolling.
2. **Combat Dice Roll**: Roll the indicated number of combat dice (faces: 0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2).
   * **Lente Preciso**: Any rolled face showing 0 is automatically converted to 1.
   * **Flauta Mágica**: Once per turn, you may reroll your lowest die if it shows 0 or 1.
   * **White Dice Criticals**: Certain attacks (*Aguijón Perforante, Mandíbula Letal*) convert each 0-face into a critical strike (+2 damage).
3. **Luck Token Spend (🍀)**: You may spend 1 Luck Token before applying damage to add **+1 to final damage**.
4. **Weakness Check (+2 Damage)**: Check if the attack's elemental type matches the defender's Weakness tag:
   * **Elemental Weakness Chart**:
     * Grass is weak to Fire.
     * Fire is weak to Water.
     * Water is weak to Electric.
     * Electric is weak to Ground.
     * Ground is weak to Grass.
     * Flying is weak to Electric.
     * Rock is weak to Water.
     * Bug is weak to Fire.
     * Dragon is weak to Ice.
     * Ice is weak to Fire.
   * If an attack hits a weakness, it gains an automatic **+2 Damage Bonus**.
5. **Item & Status Bonuses**:
   * *Banda Fuerza*: +1 damage.
   * *Colmillo Afilado*: +2 damage if attacking first.
   * *Red's Passive*: +1 damage if user HP $\le$ 50%.
   * *Charge Move*: +3 damage if the previous turn used a charge attack (*Carga Ígnea*).
6. **Defensive Reductions**:
   * *Chaleco Defensivo*: Reduces incoming attack damage by 1 (minimum 1 damage dealt).
   * *Shields*: Moves like *Pedrada Defensiva* place a shield absorbing 2 damage from the next attack received.
7. **Apply Damage to Defender**: Subtract final damage from the defending monster's HP.

##### Step 4: Survival Items & Fainting
* **Baya Aranja (Oran Berry)**: When a monster drops to 0 HP or below, the berry consumes itself immediately, prevents the faint, and **heals it to 3 HP**.
* **Cinta Focus (Focus Sash)**: If an attack would faint the monster from a conscious state, it survives with **1 HP** (1 use per combat).
* **Casco Dentado (Rocky Helmet)**: Each time the wearer is hit by an attack, the attacker suffers **1 reflected damage**.
* **Fainting**: If a creature reaches 0 HP and has no survival items, it faints:
  * If the defeated creature belonged to the opponent, you may immediately **teach its primary attack** to one of your team's monsters!
  * If the defender has remaining conscious creatures on the bench, the next conscious creature enters the battle.
  * If all 3 creatures on one side faint, the battle ends immediately.

##### Step 5: Second Attack & Status Phase
* If the defending creature survived the initial strike, it executes its attack against the first attacker.
* **End-of-Turn Status Effects**:
  * **Poison**: Deals 1 unavoidable damage at the end of each turn to the poisoned monster.
  * **Somnolence**: The affected monster suffers +1 damage from all incoming attacks.

##### Step 6: Post-Combat Restoration
Regardless of whether you won or were defeated:
* **Full Healing**: All creatures on both sides immediately restore **100% of their Maximum HP** and all status conditions are cured.
* **Consolation Prize**: Even if you lose, you receive the full coin reward of the battle.

---

#### 5.6 Turn Conclusion
After completing your combat, capture, or shop visit:
* Discard temporary turn modifiers.
* Play passes clockwise to the next player.

---

### 6. Cleanup & Round Maintenance

At the end of each round (after all players have taken their turns):
1. **Advance Round Tracker**: Move the round marker to the next space on the round track.
2. **Shop Maintenance**: Ensure the River Shop holds exactly 8 unique item cards.
3. **Deck Replenishment**: If the Environment Draw Deck is ever depleted, shuffle its discard pile to form a new draw deck.

---

### 7. End of the Game

The expedition phase of the game concludes when either of the following two conditions is met:

1. **3 Badges Earned**: The exact moment any player defeats their 3rd Gym Leader and earns their 3rd Gym Badge, exploration stops.
2. **Round 10 Ends**: If Round 10 concludes and no player has acquired 3 badges, exploration ends automatically.

When triggered, players retain all their creatures, badges, coins, luck tokens, and equipped items, proceeding directly to the **Grand Tournament**.

---

### 8. The Grand Tournament and Determining the Champion

The absolute winner of Monster Journeys is decided by resolving the Grand Tournament in the format matching your player count:

#### Format 1: Solo Play (1 Player) — The Elite Gauntlet
In solo play, you must overcome a consecutive gauntlet against **3 Elite Champions** in 3v3 battles:
1. **Match 1**: Champion Bruno (*Colosoroca, Gólemita, Sylvanor*).
2. **Match 2**: Champion Cynthia (*Dragón Coralino, Fénixdor, Gyarador*).
3. **Match 3 (Grand Final)**: Master Legend (*Leviatán Marino, Raidracos, Reina Vespa*).
* **Victory**: Defeating all 3 champions proclaims you regional Champion. Losing any match results in defeat.

#### Format 2: 2 Players — Best-of-3 Series
The two players face each other in a Best-of-3 series of 3v3 battles:
* **Match 1**: Player 1 vs Player 2.
* **Match 2**: Player 1 vs Player 2.
* **Match 3 (if tied 1–1)**: Tiebreaker deciding match.
* **Victory**: The first player to reach **2 victories** wins the tournament and the game.

#### Format 3: 3 Players — Triangular Championship
A round-robin tournament where every player battles every other player once:
* **Round 1**: Player 1 vs Player 2.
* **Round 2**: Player 2 vs Player 3.
* **Round 3**: Player 3 vs Player 1.
* **Victory**: The player with the most victories is crowned Champion. If a three-way tie occurs (1 win each), the player with the highest total remaining HP across their battles wins.

#### Format 4: 4 Players — Playoff Bracket
A single-elimination playoff bracket with a bronze final:
* **Semifinal 1**: Player 1 vs Player 4.
* **Semifinal 2**: Player 2 vs Player 3.
* **Bronze Match**: Loser of SF1 vs Loser of SF2.
* **Grand Final**: Winner of SF1 vs Winner of SF2.
* **Victory**: The winner of the Grand Final is crowned the Grand Champion of Monster Journeys.
