# Monster Journeys — Official Rulebook

**A Tabletop Board Game of Exploration, Monster Catching, and Tactical Combat**  
*For 1 to 4 players.*

---

### Introduction & Premise

In **Monster Journeys**, you take on the role of an ambitious trainer exploring an uncharted region of diverse biomes: dense forests, rugged mountains, open plains, winding freshwater rivers, and roaring ocean waters. Throughout your expedition, you will lay down territory cards to discover new routes, track and capture wild elemental monsters, learn ancestral combat techniques, purchase strategic equipment from the river market, and challenge adaptive Gym Leaders.

Your ultimate goal is to prove your tactical prowess by collecting Gym Badges, awakening legendary expansions, and triumphing in the climactic **Grand Tournament** to become the regional Champion.

### 1. Components

#### 1.1 The Regional Map Board & Nodes
* **1 Regional Node Map** consisting of **17 interconnected location nodes**:
  * **4 Starting Corner Nodes** (Capacity: 1 card each):
    * Southwest (SW): *Ciudad Hoja*
    * Northwest (NW): *Ciudad Pico*
    * Northeast (NE): *Ciudad Puerto*
    * Southeast (SE): *Ciudad Flama*
  * **3 Central Crossroads / Hubs** (Capacity: 2 cards each):
    * *Encrucijada Poniente* (West Crossroads)
    * *Gran Plaza Central* (Grand Central Plaza)
    * *Cruce del Alba* (Dawn Crossing)
  * **10 Regional Route & Dead-End Nodes** (Capacity: 1 card each):
    * *Cueva Origen*, *Valle Rocoso*, *Fosa Abisal*, *Ruta Poniente*, *Bahía Bruma*, *Ruta Septentrional*, *Pico Nevado*, *Meseta Oriental*, *Ruta Marina Este*, *Arrecife del Sol*.

#### 1.2 Location & City Cards
* **4 Gym City Cards**:
  * *Ciudad Hoja* (Leaf City — Herbaceous Gym, Leader Erika)
  * *Ciudad Pico* (Peak City — Summit Gym, Leader Brock)
  * *Ciudad Puerto* (Port City — Surge Gym, Leader Misty)
  * *Ciudad Flama* (Flame City — Volcano Gym, Leader Blaine)
* **17 Environment Cards** across 5 distinct biomes:
  * **4 Forest Cards (Bosque)**: *Bosque de las Bayas*, *Bazar del Claro*, *Arboleda de Sabiduría*, *Santuario del Druida*.
  * **4 Mountain Cards (Montaña)**: *Mina de Piedra Oro*, *Refugio de los Picos*, *Nido del Águila*, *Cueva de los Fósiles*.
  * **4 Plains Cards (Pradera)**: *Molino de los Vientos*, *Feria Ambulante*, *Establo de los Relámpagos*, *Llanura de Duelos*.
  * **3 Freshwater Cards (Agua Dulce)**: *Cascada Cristalina*, *Muelle del Pescador*, *Laguna de los Lotos*.
  * **2 Saltwater Cards (Agua Salada — Tier 1)**: *Arrecife Resplandeciente*, *Navío Naufragado*.
  * **1 Saltwater Special Card (Agua Salada — Tier 3 Expansion)**: *Vórtice Legendario*.

#### 1.3 Monster Cards (50 Cards Total)
Monsters are categorized into 4 capture tiers and 10 elemental types (*Grass, Fire, Water, Electric, Ground, Flying, Rock, Bug, Dragon, Ice*):
* **40 Standard Monsters** (8 per biome: 2 Tier 1, 2 Tier 2, 4 Tier 3).
* **10 Tier 4 Legendary Monsters** (Awakened via Tier 4 expansion): *Yggdrassor (Grass), Soberanocoris (Bug), Geotitán (Rock), Terragón (Ground), Aethelgard (Flying), Glaciodón (Ice), Fulgoraptor (Electric), Piroclasto (Fire), Hydragón Marino (Water), Bahamut Abisal (Dragon)*.

#### 1.4 Item Cards (20 Cards Total)
* **4 Capture Tools**: *Pokeball Básica* (Tier 1, Cost 4), *Superball Reforzada* (Tier 2, Cost 8), *Ultraball Avanzada* (Tier 3, Cost 14), *Masterball Suprema* (Tier 4, Cost 22).
* **9 Combat Items**: *Banda Fuerza* (Cost 6), *Chaleco Defensivo* (Cost 6), *Garra Rápida* (Cost 5), *Cinta Focus* (Cost 8), *Lente Preciso* (Cost 7), *Colmillo Afilado* (Cost 6), *Baya Aranja / Oran Berry* (Cost 4), *Casco Dentado* (Cost 6), *Pesa Recia* (Cost 7).
* **7 Utility Items**: *Botas Ligeras* (Cost 5), *Incienso Silvestre* (Cost 5), *Mapa de Rutas* (Cost 5), *Flauta Mágica* (Cost 6), *Moneda Amuleto* (Cost 6), *Telescopio Explorador* (Cost 7), *Imán de Tesoros* (Cost 7).

#### 1.5 Character Cards & Player Materials
* **4 Character Cards**:
  * **Red (El Audaz)**: Starts with 2 Coins, 3 Luck, *Candilín* (Fire Starter), *Pokeball Básica*, *Baya Aranja*. Passive: *Determinación Ardiente* (+1 damage when current HP $\le$ 50%).
  * **Blue (El Estratega)**: Starts with 2 Coins, 4 Luck, *Renacuajito* (Water Starter), *Superball Reforzada*, *Banda Fuerza*. Passive: *Primer Golpe & Fortuna* (+2 Speed on turn 1; utility shop items cost 1 less coin).
  * **Marina (La Pescadora)**: Starts with 4 Coins, 3 Luck, *Patiloto* (Water Starter), *Pokeball Básica*, *Botas Ligeras*. Passive: *Navegante Experta* (Full movement speed in water biomes; visiting a water node grants +1 Luck).
  * **Flint (El Montañero)**: Starts with 3 Coins, 3 Luck, *Hojarín* (Grass Starter), *Pokeball Básica*, *Chaleco Defensivo*. Passive: *Resistencia Rocosa* (+2 Maximum HP to all monsters on your team).
* **4 Player Pawns** (Red, Blue, Green, Purple).
* **Personal Player Boards** (Each holds up to 4 monsters: 3 active party members + 1 reserve).

#### 1.6 Dice, Tokens & Markers
* **Combat Dice**: Six-sided dice with custom values: **[0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2]**.
* **Gold Coin Tokens (🪙)**: The regional currency.
* **Luck Tokens (🍀)**: Reroll and modifier tokens.
* **4 Gym Badges**: *Medalla Hoja*, *Medalla Risco*, *Medalla Cascada*, *Medalla Volcán*.
* **Round Marker**: Tracks rounds 1 through 10.

### 2. Setting Up the Game

Follow these numbered steps in physical order:

1. **Place the Regional Board**: Lay the regional node map in the center of the table.
2. **Plant Starting Corner Cities**: Place the 4 Gym City cards on the 4 corner nodes of the board:
   * SW Corner $\rightarrow$ *Ciudad Hoja*
   * NW Corner $\rightarrow$ *Ciudad Pico*
   * NE Corner $\rightarrow$ *Ciudad Puerto*
   * SE Corner $\rightarrow$ *Ciudad Flama*
3. **Prepare the Expansion Reserves**:
   * Set aside the Tier 2, Tier 3, and Tier 4 Monster Cards into separate face-down Expansion decks.
   * Set aside the Tier 3 Environment card (*Vórtice Legendario*) into the Tier 3 expansion reserve.
4. **Prepare the Initial Environment Deck**: Shuffle all remaining Tier 1 Environment Cards into a face-down draw deck and place it beside the board.
5. **Prepare the 5 Biome Encounter Decks**: Separate the Tier 1 monster cards by biome (*Forest, Mountain, Plains, Freshwater, Saltwater*). Shuffle each into its own face-down deck near the board.
6. **Establish the River Shop**:
   * Shuffle the 20 Item Cards.
   * Deal **exactly 8 unique item cards** face-up in a row to form the River Shop display. If a duplicate item appears, return it to the deck, reshuffle, and draw until 8 unique cards are visible. Place the remaining cards face-down as the Item Draw Deck.
7. **Player Setup**:
   * Each player chooses or draws 1 Character Card.
   * Take the matching colored player pawn.
   * Take the starting Gold Coins and Luck Tokens listed on your Character Card.
   * Take your character's designated Starter Monster and starting items into your personal inventory.
   * Place your starter monster in the first active slot of your personal board.
8. **Deal Starting Location Hands**: Deal **5 Location Cards** from the Environment Deck to each player. This forms your private hand of location cards.
9. **Place Starting Pawns**:
   * Player 1 places their pawn on the SW Corner (*Ciudad Hoja*).
   * Player 2 places their pawn on the NE Corner (*Ciudad Puerto*).
   * Player 3 (if playing) places their pawn on the NW Corner (*Ciudad Pico*).
   * Player 4 (if playing) places their pawn on the SE Corner (*Ciudad Flama*).
10. **Set the Round Marker**: Place the Round Marker on space 1 of the round track. Player 1 takes the first turn.

---

### 3. Object of the Game

In Monster Journeys, you compete to assemble the strongest battle team and explore the region. You achieve this by:
* Discovering new biomes and creating strategic travel connections.
* Capturing wild creatures and equipping them with tactical items.
* Challenging Gym Leaders to earn Gym Badges.
* Unlocking global Expansion Decks that unleash higher-tier monsters and mythical locations.

#### Endgame Triggers & Victory
The exploration phase of the game ends immediately upon either of two events:
1. **Any player defeats their 3rd Gym Leader** and collects 3 Gym Badges.
2. **Round 10 concludes**.

When either trigger occurs, regular exploration ceases and all players enter the **Grand Tournament**. The player who wins the tournament is declared the winner of the game.

---

### 4. Game Round

The game takes place over a maximum of **10 rounds**. Each round proceeds as follows:

1. **Player Turns**: Beginning with Player 1 and proceeding in clockwise order, each player executes their full turn.
2. **Round Maintenance**: Once the last player has completed their turn:
   * Advance the Round Marker by 1 space on the round track.
   * If the marker moves past space 10, the exploration phase ends immediately and the Grand Tournament begins (see [Section 8](#8-the-grand-tournament-and-determining-the-champion)).
   * Otherwise, the next round begins with Player 1.

---

### 5. Player's Turn

On your turn, follow this execution sequence in order:

* **5.1 Start of Turn Maintenance**
* **5.2 Movement & Map Placement**
* **5.3 Location Action (Optional)**
* **5.4 Main Encounter / Gym / Shop Action (Mandatory turn-ender)**
* **5.5 End of Turn**

---

#### 5.1 Start of Turn Maintenance
1. **Gain Item Incomes**: Resolve any passive start-of-turn items in your inventory.
   > **Example**: If you possess the *Imán de Tesoros* (Treasure Magnet), gain 1 Gold Coin (🪙) from the supply.
2. **Reset Movement Points**: Gain **1 Movement Point** for the turn.
   > **Exception**: If you hold or have equipped the *Botas Ligeras* (Light Boots), your base movement for the turn is **2 Movement Points**.
3. **Refill Location Hand**: If you have fewer than 5 cards in your location hand, draw cards from the Environment Deck one at a time until your hand holds **exactly 5 cards**.
   * **Luck Draft Option**: If you have enabled the *Draft on Draw* mode and possess at least 1 Luck Token (🍀), you may spend 1 Luck Token to reveal the top 3 cards of the Environment Deck, choose 1 to keep, and discard the other 2.

---

#### 5.2 Movement & Map Placement
During your movement step, you must execute one of the following movement options using your movement points:

##### Option A: Travel Existing Routes
Move your pawn along connected path lines to any discovered node (a node already containing at least 1 card). Each step along a line costs 1 Movement Point. You may only move into nodes that are already discovered.

##### Option B: Discover a New Zone (Place Location Card)
If you are adjacent via a path line to an empty node slot (or a multi-card crossroads node with remaining capacity):
1. Choose 1 Location Card from your hand.
2. Place it face-up on the target node.
3. Move your pawn onto that newly placed card, spending 1 Movement Point.
4. **Placement Luck Bonus**: Check the total number of route connections on the node where you just placed the card:
   * If the node has **exactly 1 connection** (a dead-end/extremity), immediately gain **+1 Luck Token (🍀)**.
   * If the node has 2 or more connections, no luck bonus is awarded.
5. If you possess the *Mapa de Rutas* (Route Map), immediately gain **+1 Gold Coin (🪙)**.
6. Refill your location hand back to 5 cards from the Environment Deck.

##### Option C: Fast Travel Between Cities
If your pawn is currently standing on any City node (*Ciudad Hoja, Ciudad Pico, Ciudad Puerto, Ciudad Flama*):
* You may spend your movement to fast-travel directly to any other discovered City node on the map, regardless of physical path distance.
* Fast-traveling between cities consumes both your movement and your action for the turn.

##### Overworld Monster Abilities (Movement & Utility)
Before or during movement, you may activate the overworld ability of one of your active monsters:
* **Excavación (Ground)**: Gain +1 additional Movement Point for the turn.
* **Nado Veloz (Water)**: Movement is set to 2 when moving across water biomes (*Freshwater* or *Saltwater*).
* **Vuelo Rápido (Flying)**: Teleport your pawn directly to any discovered City node from any position on the board.
* **Calor de Campamento (Fire)**: Heal 3 HP to all monsters in your party and gain 1 Gold Coin.
* **Sobrecarga (Electric) / Romperrocas (Rock)**: Gain 1 Gold Coin.
* **Rastreo Silvestre (Grass)**: Reveal 1 additional card during wild searches this turn.
* **Red de Seda (Bug)**: Your next capture this turn treats your capture tool tier as +1 higher.

---

#### 5.3 Location Action (Optional)
If the card on your current node features a special Location Action, you may resolve it.

* **Single-Use Restriction**: Each specific location card's action may be used **only once per player per game**. Mark the card with your player token to record that you have utilized its benefit.

##### Notable Location Actions:
* **Descanso en Casa (Starting Town / Home)**: Fully heal all your monsters to 100% HP and gain +1 Luck (🍀).
* **Bosque de las Bayas**: Gain 1 free *Baya Aranja* (Oran Berry) card and +1 Luck (🍀).
* **Arboleda de Sabiduría**: Spend 1 Luck (🍀) to teach 1 Ancestral Attack (*Danza de Espinas, Ignición Mística, Chorro Torrencial, Electrochoque, Muralla de Granito, Veneno Arácnido*) to one of your monsters.
* **Santuario del Druida**: Permanently grant +1 Maximum HP to one of your monsters and gain 1 Gold Coin (🪙).
* **Muelle del Pescador (Travesía en Barca)**: Sail directly to any discovered node on the entire map board.
* **Feria Ambulante (Mobile Market)**: Opens the River Shop immediately with a 1-coin discount on all items.
* **Vórtice Legendario (Tier 3 Mythic Area)**: Spend 2 Luck (🍀) to summon and search among the Tier 4 Legendary Monsters.
* **Trading Locations**: Exchange indicated coins for luck, or luck for coins, as printed on the card.

---

#### 5.4 Main Action (Turn-Ending Step)
After completing your movement and any optional location action, you must choose **one** of the following primary actions. Resolving this action concludes your turn.

---

##### Action 1: Search Wild Monsters (Non-City Biomes)
You cannot search for wild monsters while standing in a City. While in a wild biome (*Forest, Mountain, Plains, Freshwater, Saltwater*):
1. **Reveal Encounters**: Draw **3 monster cards** from the matching Biome Encounter Deck and lay them face-up.
   * Add +1 card if you activated a Grass overworld ability.
   * Add +1 card if you own the *Incienso Silvestre* (Wild Incense).
   * Add +1 card if you own the *Telescopio Explorador* (Explorer Telescope).
2. **Redraw Option (Luck)**: Before choosing an outcome, you may spend **1 Luck Token (🍀)** to discard all revealed monsters and draw a completely new set of cards.
3. **Select One Outcome**:
   * **Capture**: Choose 1 revealed monster to capture. You must possess a Capture Tool item in your inventory whose Tier is greater than or equal to the monster's Capture Tier:
     * *Pokeball Básica*: Captures Tier 1.
     * *Superball Reforzada*: Captures Tier 1–2.
     * *Ultraball Avanzada*: Captures Tier 1–3.
     * *Masterball Suprema*: Captures any Tier (1–4).
     * *Placement*: Place the captured monster onto your personal board (up to 4 monsters: 3 active + 1 reserve). If your board already holds 4 monsters, send the captured monster to your personal storage.
   * **Teach an Attack**: Select 1 revealed monster. You may teach its primary attack directly to any monster on your team. Each monster can know up to 3 attacks. If a monster already knows 3 attacks, replace the secondary attack.
   * **Flee / Dismiss**: Conclude the search without capturing or learning an attack.

---

##### Action 2: Challenge a Gym Leader (City Nodes Only)
While standing on a City node whose Gym you have not yet defeated:
1. Announce your challenge to the resident Gym Leader.
2. **Adaptive Gym Scaling**: Gym Leaders dynamically adjust their team strength based on how many total Gym Badges you currently possess:
   * **0 Badges Defeated**: The Gym Leader deploys a **Tier 1 Team** (2 Tier 1 monsters). Victory reward: **3 Coins, 1 Luck, Gym Badge**.
   * **1 Badge Defeated**: The Gym Leader deploys a **Tier 2 Team** (2 Tier 2 monsters). Victory reward: **4 Coins, 1 Luck, Gym Badge**.
   * **2 Badges Defeated**: The Gym Leader deploys a **Tier 3 Team** (3 Tier 3 monsters). Victory reward: **6 Coins, 2 Luck, Gym Badge**.
3. **Execute the Combat**: Resolve the battle following the complete combat rules in [Section 5.5](#55-in-depth-combat-rules).
4. **Resolution & Rewards**:
   * **If Victorious**: Collect the designated Gold Coins, Luck Tokens, and the City's Gym Badge.
   * **If Defeated**: You still collect the **full coin reward** of the battle as a consolation prize to rebuild your team!
   * **Global Expansion Unlock**:
     * Defeating the world's 1st Gym unlocks the **Tier 2 Expansion** (Tier 2 monsters are shuffled into biome decks).
     * Defeating the world's 2nd Gym unlocks the **Tier 3 Expansion** (Tier 3 monsters + *Vórtice Legendario* are added).
     * Defeating the world's 3rd Gym unlocks the **Tier 4 Expansion** (Tier 4 Legendaries awaken).

---

##### Action 3: Visit the River Shop (City Nodes Only)
You may only access the shop while standing in a City node (or via the *Feria Ambulante* location action).
1. **Browse Display**: You may buy any item from the 8 face-up cards in the River Shop by paying its Gold Coin cost into the supply.
2. **Immediate Refill**: Whenever an item is purchased, immediately draw a card from the Item Deck to replace it. The River Shop must always display **8 unique items** without duplicates.
3. **Equip Items**: You may freely equip, unequip, or swap items between your inventory and your monsters outside of battle:
   * Each monster may hold **at most 1 equipped item**.
   * Equipping *Pesa Recia* (+3 Max HP) immediately increases current and maximum HP by 3. Unequipping it reduces maximum HP by 3.

---

##### Action 4: Pass
If you cannot or choose not to take any other action, conclude your turn.

---

#### 5.5 In-Depth Combat Rules
Combat occurs 1-on-1 between active monsters, with both sides bringing up to 3 monsters into the battle arena.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------+
|                       COMBAT ARENA                        |
|                                                           |
|  [Opponent Active Monster]      HP: [======    ] 12/18   |
|                                 Speed: 6 | Weak: Water    |
|                                                           |
|  [Your Active Monster]          HP: [==========] 15/15   |
|                                 Speed: 8 | Weak: Fire     |
|                                                           |
|  Options: [Attack Move 1]  [Attack Move 2]  [Switch Mon]  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------+
```

##### Step 1: Turn Decision
Each combat turn, you must choose either to **Attack** or to **Switch Monsters**:

##### Step 2A: Switching Monsters
* You may switch your active monster for any conscious reserve monster in your active party of 3.
* **Switch Penalty**: Switching absorbs your entire turn. The incoming monster enters the arena and **immediately receives the opponent's attack without dealing damage** that round!
  > **Exception**: Attacks with the **Pivot Switch** effect (*Voltiocambio, Ida y Vuelta, Giro Marino*) allow you to attack and execute a tactical switch immediately afterward with zero damage penalty.

##### Step 2B: Attacking & Initiative
If you choose to attack, select 1 attack from your active monster's known attacks. The opponent selects an attack simultaneously.
1. **Determine Initiative**: Compare the current Speed attribute of both active monsters:
   * The monster with higher Speed strikes first. Ties are broken randomly.
   * **Paralysis**: A paralyzed monster has its Speed reduced to **0**.
   * **Item Modifiers**: *Garra Rápida* grants +2 Speed. Blue's character passive grants +2 Speed on combat turn 1.

##### Step 3: Resolving an Attack
Total damage dealt by an attack is calculated as:
$$\text{Total Damage} = \text{Base Damage} + \text{Combat Dice Roll} + \text{Bonuses} - \text{Defenses}$$

1. **Base Damage**: Stated on the attack card.
   * *Speed Damage*: If the attack has the `speed_damage` effect (*Electroimpulso, Giro Rápido*), base damage equals the attacker's Speed attribute.
   * *Fixed Damage*: If the attack has the `fixed_damage` effect (*Furia Dragón, Fijación Sísmica*), it deals guaranteed damage and ignores dice rolling.
2. **Combat Dice Roll**: Roll the indicated number of combat dice (faces: 0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2).
   * **Lente Preciso**: Any rolled face showing 0 is automatically converted to 1.
   * **Flauta Mágica**: Once per turn, you may reroll your lowest die if it shows 0 or 1.
   * **White Dice Critical**: Certain attacks (*Aguijón Perforante, Mandíbula Letal*) turn 0-faces into critical strikes (+2 damage per zero).
3. **Luck Point Spend (🍀)**: You may spend **1 Luck Token** before damage is applied to add **+1 damage** to your attack.
4. **Weakness Check (+2 Damage)**: Check if the attack's elemental type matches the defender's specific Weakness tag (or standard type chart weakness):
   * **Type Weakness Chart**:
     * Grass is weak to Fire.
     * Fire is weak to Water.
     * Water is weak to Electric.
     * Electric is weak to Ground.
     * Ground is weak to Grass.
     * Flying is weak to Electric.
     * Rock is weak to Water.
     * Bug is weak to Fire.
     * Dragon is weak to Ice.
     * Ice is weak to Fire.
   * If an attack strikes a weakness, it gains an automatic **+2 Damage Bonus**.
5. **Item & Ability Bonuses**:
   * *Banda Fuerza*: +1 damage.
   * *Colmillo Afilado*: +2 damage if striking first.
   * *Red's Passive*: +1 damage if user HP $\le$ 50%.
   * *Charged Attack*: +3 damage if previous turn used a charge attack (*Carga Ígnea*).
6. **Defensive Reductions**:
   * *Chaleco Defensivo*: Reduces incoming attack damage by 1 (minimum 1 damage dealt).
   * *Shield*: Attacks like *Pedrada Defensiva* erect a shield reducing the next incoming attack by 2.
7. **Apply Damage to Defender**: Reduce defender HP by the final damage total.

##### Step 4: Survival Items & Fainting
* **Baya Aranja (Oran Berry)**: When a monster's HP reaches 0, the berry consumes itself immediately, prevents the faint, and **restores the monster to 3 HP**.
* **Cinta Focus (Focus Sash)**: When an attack would reduce the monster from conscious to 0 HP, it survives with **1 HP** (1 use per combat).
* **Casco Dentado (Rocky Helmet)**: Whenever the wearer is hit by an attack, the attacker suffers **1 reflected damage**.
* **Fainting**: If a monster drops to 0 HP and has no survival items, it is knocked out.
  * If the defeated monster was an opponent, you may immediately **teach its primary attack** to one of your party monsters!
  * If the defender has remaining conscious monsters on their bench of 3, the next monster enters the battlefield.
  * If all 3 monsters of a team faint, the battle ends immediately.

##### Step 5: Second Strike & Status Phase
* If the defending monster survived, it executes its chosen attack against the first attacker.
* **End-of-Turn Status Effects**:
  * **Poison (Veneno)**: Inflicts 1 unblockable damage at the end of every turn.
  * **Somnolence (Somnolencia)**: The affected monster receives +1 damage from all incoming attacks.

##### Step 6: Post-Combat Restoration
Regardless of whether you won or lost:
* **Full Healing**: All monsters on both sides immediately recover **100% of their Maximum HP** and all status conditions are completely cured.
* **Loss Prize**: Even if you are defeated, you receive the full coin reward of the battle.

---

#### 5.6 End of Turn
Once your main encounter, gym battle, or shop visit concludes:
* Discard any temporary turn bonuses.
* Turn passes clockwise to the next player.

---

### 6. Cleanup & Round Maintenance

At the end of each round (after all players have taken their turns):
1. **Advance Round Tracker**: Move the Round Marker to the next space on the round track.
2. **Shop Maintenance**: Ensure the River Shop holds exactly 8 unique items. If any empty slots exist, refill from the Item Draw Deck.
3. **Deck Replenishment**: If the Environment Draw Deck is ever depleted, shuffle the environment discard pile to form a new draw deck.

---

### 7. End of the Game

The exploration and journeying phase of the game terminates under either of the following two conditions:

1. **3 Gym Badges Earned**: The exact moment any player defeats their 3rd Gym Leader and claims their 3rd Gym Badge, the game triggers the finale.
2. **Round 10 Concluded**: If Round 10 ends and no player has acquired 3 badges, the game triggers the finale immediately.

When triggered, the map phase stops. Players retain all their acquired monsters, badges, coins, luck, and equipment, and advance directly to the **Grand Tournament**.

---

### 8. The Grand Tournament and Determining the Champion

The winner of Monster Journeys is determined by resolving the Grand Tournament using the format matching your player count:

#### Format 1: Solo Play (1 Player) — The Elite Gauntlet
In solo play, you must run an elimination gauntlet against **3 Elite NPC Champions** in consecutive 3v3 battles:
1. **Match 1**: Champion Bruno (*Colosoroca, Gólemita, Sylvanor*).
2. **Match 2**: Champion Cynthia (*Dragón Coralino, Fénixdor, Gyarador*).
3. **Match 3 (Grand Final)**: Master Legend (*Leviatán Marino, Raidracos, Reina Vespa*).
* **Victory**: Defeating all 3 champions crowns you the Champion. Failing any match results in defeat.

#### Format 2: 2 Players — Best-of-3 Series
The two players face each other in a Best-of-3 series of 3v3 battles:
* **Match 1**: Player 1 vs Player 2.
* **Match 2**: Player 1 vs Player 2.
* **Match 3 (if tied 1–1)**: Tiebreaker Decider.
* **Victory**: The first player to win **2 matches** is crowned the Champion.

#### Format 3: 3 Players — Triangular Championship
A round-robin tournament where every player battles every other player once:
* Match 1: Player 1 vs Player 2.
* Match 2: Player 2 vs Player 3.
* Match 3: Player 3 vs Player 1.
* **Victory**: The player with the most match victories wins the game. If all 3 players tie with 1 win each, the player with the highest total remaining HP across their battles wins.

#### Format 4: 4 Players — Championship Playoffs
A four-player seeded playoff bracket:
* **Semifinal 1**: Player 1 vs Player 4.
* **Semifinal 2**: Player 2 vs Player 3.
* **Bronze Match**: Loser of SF1 vs Loser of SF2 (competing for 3rd place).
* **Grand Final**: Winner of SF1 vs Winner of SF2.
* **Victory**: The winner of the Grand Final is crowned the Grand Champion of Monster Journeys!
